## Summary

Differential geometry, centred on **Lie groupoids and Lie algebroids** and their
role in **Poisson geometry** and **singular spaces**. Recurring themes:
integration problems (Lie's third theorem for algebroids, singular foliations),
stacks and Morita equivalence, diffeology, and groupoid cohomology. Some
overlap with operator algebras (convolution algebras of groupoids) and with
higher structures (double groupoids, 2-groups, simplicial methods).

- PhD 2018, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (advisor Rui Loja Fernandes),
  thesis *Stacks in Poisson Geometry*.
- Currently Lecturer, Indiana University. Previously Postdoctoral Lecturer,
  Washington University in St. Louis. (Public WashU page is out of date.)
- arXiv primary classes: math.DG, math.SG; secondary math.OA, math.AG.
