# Deep dive on the 30 positions (2026-10-06)

Research notes per position: faculty overlap with Joel's work, pay evidence,
and anything unusual about the application. Deadlines, letters and document
codes live in `tracker.csv`. Extra document codes used there: `AMS` AMS cover
sheet, `TR` transcripts, `TE` teaching evaluations, `TD` teaching dossier,
`LS` leadership statement, `SIG` half-page significant-contributions statement,
`SSS` student success statement, `SAMPLE` sample teaching activity.

**Pay evidence.** "AAUP" = average assistant-professor salary at the
institution, all departments, 2025–26 ([AAUP Appendix I](https://www.aaup.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/ARES-preliminary-appendixes-2025-26.pdf)).
It is an average over everyone at that rank, so a new math hire is usually at
or below it. "Stated" = range in the ad.

**Connection strength.** Strong = someone whose work is directly on Lie
groupoids/algebroids, Poisson geometry, foliations, stacks, or groupoid
operator algebras. Some = symplectic/differential geometry, operator algebras,
higher categories. "(unverified)" = from search results only; the department
site blocked automated access.
